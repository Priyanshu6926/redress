# Arkiv Reach & Roadmap

---

## 1. Who Uses Redress on Arkiv
- **DeFi Borrowers**: Querying the public ledger to see if their liquidations were flagged as oracle mispricings and whether payouts are available.
- **Protocol Risk Officers & DAOs**: Auditing restitution calculations, verifying that historical repayments match independent consensus prices.
- **Underwriters & Insurance Vaults**: Evaluating liquidation risks and automated restitution reliability across lending pools.

## 2. Post-Mumbai Deployment Roadmap
- **Mainnet Launch**: Deploying Redress Sentinel nodes indexing Aave v3, Morpho Blue, and Compound III markets.
- **Cross-Rollup Indexing**: Expanding Arkiv entity tracking to Base, Arbitrum, and Optimism lending markets.
- **DAO Governance Integration**: Providing decentralized evidence packs that directly interface with protocol treasury timelocks.
