# Reach

## Who would use this

| User | What they do |
|---|---|
| A liquidated borrower | Enters an address and sees the verdict, the evidence and the payout |
| A DAO delegate | Checks an incident's numbers against the on-chain record before voting |
| A risk team | Filters verdicts by size and block to study an incident |
| A wallet or dashboard | Queries Arkiv directly to show a user their verdicts |

## Who uses it today

Nobody yet. It is a hackathon demo on testnets, built around one real incident: 33 addresses and 47 liquidation events.

## Does it ship after Mumbai?

**Undecided.** The team has not made this decision, so it is left open here rather than promised.

If it continues, the steps are:

1. Deploy the workflow on the CRE network, which needs Early Access.
2. Get an independent audit.
3. Find a protocol or DAO willing to fund and whitelist a vault.
